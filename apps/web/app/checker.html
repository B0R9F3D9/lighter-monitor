Export Trades from Lighter

Lighter Trade Exporter
Method A: Exporter Bookmarklet (1-Click)
1 Drag the button above to your browser's Bookmarks bar (or click "Copy Code" to add manually).
2 Open app.lighter.xyz or robinhoodchain.lighter.xyz and log in.
3 Click the bookmarklet in your toolbar. It will fetch all historical trades and automatically save a JSON file.
Method B: Zero-Trust Manual F12 Network Export
1. Open Lighter trading dashboard and press F12.
2. Switch to Network tab and filter for trades.
3. Right-click the request, select Copy → Copy response, and click "Paste JSON" here.
